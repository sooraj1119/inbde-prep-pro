8. **1133**:
   - Topic: Anterior implant crown dark line and "sensitivity to cold".
   - Clinical derivation: Implants lack pulp tissue and cannot experience cold sensitivity! A thin biotype with metal abutment causes dark line show-through, but the cold sensitivity symptom in the stem is biologically impossible.
   - Designated Answer: B.
   - Clinical Verdict: FLAG (physiologically flawed stem/distractor). Format: compliant (or flagged).

9. **1134**:
